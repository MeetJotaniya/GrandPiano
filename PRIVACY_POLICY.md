# Privacy Policy for Grand Piano

**Effective date: September 28, 2026**

Grand Piano ("the App") is a virtual piano application developed by Meet Patel. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## 1. Information We Collect

Grand Piano does **not** require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or account credentials.

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 2. Audio and Recordings

Grand Piano allows you to play piano notes, record your playing, and export recordings.

Recordings are processed locally on your device. The App does not upload your piano recordings, audio files, or generated video files to a server.

When you choose to share or save an exported recording, Android's or another platform's system sharing/storage functionality may be used. The destination you choose may have its own privacy policy and data practices.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Because Grand Piano does not collect personal information, it does not use personal information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or user tracking.

The App processes piano sounds and recordings locally only to provide its features, including:

- Playing the 88-key virtual piano
- Recording piano notes
- Playing recorded performances
- Exporting recordings as audio
- Exporting recordings as video
- Sharing exported files using the device's sharing functionality

## 4. Data Storage

Temporary files required to create exported recordings may be created on your device during the export process.

Exported files are stored or shared according to the location and action you select using your device's system functionality.

Grand Piano does not operate a remote server for storing your recordings or personal information.

You can remove locally stored App data by using your device's app settings or uninstalling the App. Files that you explicitly exported or shared outside the App may remain in the destination you selected and must be removed separately.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Grand Piano does not intentionally send personal information to third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

The App may use platform-provided functionality, such as Android's system sharing mechanisms, when you choose to share an exported file. Those platform components are governed by the applicable platform provider's terms and privacy practices.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Grand Piano does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Since the App does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information, it is designed to minimize data collection.

## 7. Security

Grand Piano does not maintain a remote database containing user personal information or recordings.

However, no software or device can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use the normal security features provided by your device and operating system.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this location with a new effective date.

## 9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Grand Piano's privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the Grand Piano project's GitHub repository:

https://github.com/MeetJotaniya/GrandPiano
